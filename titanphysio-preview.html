Our approach

A clinical and movement-based approach.

We combine evidence-based physiotherapy with a deep understanding of human movement to treat, prevent and optimise physical function.

  1. 01

    Assessment

    We listen, observe how you move and find where the problem actually starts — not only where it hurts.

  2. 02

    Individualised treatment

    Hands-on therapy, targeted techniques and exercise, combined in a plan designed for your body and your goals.

  3. 03

    Long-term performance

    You leave with the tools to keep moving well: a home programme, clear progressions and prevention.

Services

What we treat, and how.

  • Manual mobilisation to relieve pain and improve mobility of joints and soft tissue.

  • Treatment and prevention of sports injuries, from the first days after injury to a safe return to training and competition.

  • Strengthening and rehabilitation of the pelvic floor, in a discreet and respectful setting.

  • Relief of muscle pain using fine needles, with or without electrical stimulation.

  • Ultrasound-guided nerve stimulation used in the treatment of a range of conditions.

  • Muscle relaxation and improved circulation, as a treatment in itself or as part of your plan.

  • Advice on healthy habits, nutrition and physical activity that supports your recovery.

Costs & insurance

How your treatment is paid for.

A

With a doctor's prescription

Physiotherapy is covered by basic health insurance (KVG/LAMal) when a doctor prescribes it. One prescription covers up to 9 sessions, and the first session should take place within five weeks of the prescription.

Your usual deductible and co-payment apply.

B

Without a prescription

You can book directly as a self-paying patient. Some supplementary insurance policies reimburse part of the cost — check the conditions of your policy.

Self-pay rates: to confirm

C

Accident cover

After an accident, physiotherapy is usually covered by your accident insurance (UVG/LAA via your employer, or your health insurer). Bring your claim number to the first session.

Wording to confirm with Aaron

FAQ

Good to know before your first visit.

Do I need a doctor's prescription?

Not to book. You need one if you want basic health insurance to cover the treatment; otherwise you can come as a self-paying patient.

How long is a session?

To confirm with Aaron.

What should I bring?

Your prescription (if you have one), your insurance card, any relevant reports or scans, and comfortable clothes.

Is dry needling painful?

You may feel a brief twitch or local ache in the muscle. Aaron will explain the technique beforehand and you can stop at any time.
